## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Download the GloxMed `.zip` file.
2. Extract the contents to a folder.
3. Open the extracted folder.
4. Run `GloxMed.exe`.
5. Configure your OpenRouter API key.
6. Select an available AI model.
7. Create a patient profile.
8. Upload a supported medical document.
9. Add optional symptoms or additional context.
10. Generate your MediNote or MediReport.

> Keep the files included with the downloaded package together in the same folder.

## 📄 Supported Documents

GloxMed currently supports:

* `.pdf`
* `.docx`

> Image-only or scanned PDFs may require OCR before they can be processed.

## 🔐 Privacy & Data Handling

GloxMed is designed as a local desktop application.

Patient profiles and generated reports are stored locally on the user's computer.

Medical documents are processed locally for text extraction. Extracted document information and user-provided context may be sent to the AI model selected through OpenRouter.

> Users should review the privacy and data-handling policies of the AI service they choose to use.

---

## 🤖 OpenRouter Setup

GloxMed requires an **OpenRouter API key** for AI-powered processing.

The application allows the API key to be configured directly inside GloxMed.

Available AI models can be detected automatically, including supported free models.

---

## 📑 Generated Reports

### MediNote

A simplified explanation of the uploaded medical document.

### MediReport

A structured report containing relevant patient information, findings, symptoms, clarification points, and information useful for discussion with a healthcare professional.

Generated reports are stored in:

```text

```
